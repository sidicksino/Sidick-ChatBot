## Project: Calance AI
Calance AI is my AI nutrition and calorie-tracking mobile app: "Snap your food. Reach your goal." You take a photo of your meal and it turns it into calories and macros (protein, carbs, fat, plus fiber, sugar and sodium), then keeps you on a personal plan for your goal: lose, maintain or gain weight. It also has a meal library, water and weight tracking, and daily streaks, with safety limits built into weight-loss plans. Built with Expo and React Native, Clerk for sign-in, Convex as the backend, RevenueCat for subscriptions, and Google Gemini for meal-photo scanning and plans. Coming soon to iPhone on the App Store; the app subscription is $6.99 per month or $29.99 per year. One of my favorite projects. Website: https://gainlyai.vercel.app/ (the code is private).
En français : Calance AI est mon application mobile de suivi des calories par IA : on photographie son repas et l'app calcule les calories et les macros, avec un plan personnalisé. Site : https://gainlyai.vercel.app/
